What you're booking

Blues Across America is a touring show built around Bill Grisolia's voice and piano and a band that plays it loud, tight and joyful.

The set runs from deep roots blues to modern blues rock. Rooms fill up, dance floors get crowded and people line up afterward to talk to the band. Bill works the crowd onstage and off.

The show scales to the room: a full band for casino showrooms and festival stages, a smaller lineup for lounges and private events.

About Bill

Bill Grisolia is a singer, songwriter and piano player who has spent decades bringing the blues to stages across the U.S. and Europe.

Born in Kansas City and based in Long Beach, California, Bill co-led the New Blues Revolution with guitarist Chap Cooper for over a decade, playing as many as five gigs a week across Southern California. Since 2021 he has toured nationally as the Bill Grisolia Band.

His songwriting drew praise from the legendary Hal David, and he founded the New Blues Festival to give the next wave of blues artists a stage. Blues Across America takes that energy on the road.
